From first proposal to final agenda.

ChartStead is conference programming and speaker management software. It carries the work from the call for speakers to the public agenda without losing context between proposals, reviewers, speakers, messages, and the schedule.

View ChartStead Try the demo Email Tyler
Product insight

The program is one job. The tools usually are not.

Conference programming is a continuity problem. Proposals, reviews, speaker materials, messages, rooms, and the public agenda live in different places, so operators reconstruct the same facts at every handoff.

ChartStead keeps that work in one product surface. Incomplete, tentative, and unplaced state stays visible. Decisions stay private until the team is ready to communicate or publish.

System architecture

Collect, decide, prepare, then publish.

The operating principle is simple: decide privately, communicate deliberately. Course Check shows who and what will change before a choice becomes a record, a draft, or a send.

Proposal collection

Open the call and keep each proposal with its speaker details and files.

Private review

See each proposal in its track and make a private choice with the full story close by.

Speaker preparation

Give each speaker clear tasks, session details, and messages without typing the same facts twice.

Course Check

Show who and what will change before applying a decision, making a draft, or sending a message.

Agenda publication

Build the agenda as plans change, then share the final program when the team is ready.

Public proof

The marketing site is live at chartstead.com. A sample-data demo lets people inspect the workflow.

What it demonstrates

ChartStead is a workflow product and a systems case study.

For employers and clients, the signal is the judgment underneath it: name the real operator job, keep human authority in the consequential steps, and ship a focused product instead of an all-in-one event suite.
