Omamail Desktop

Mail and calendar on Tyler Mayberry’s Omarchy desktop.

Omamail Desktop is Tyler Mayberry’s personal Google OAuth configuration for the open-source Omamail plugin. It connects accounts the owner is authorized to use to a local desktop mail and calendar client. This is not a public hosted email service or the upstream project’s official website.

What it does

The desktop client displays and searches Gmail messages, manages labels and message status, composes and sends mail, and views and manages Google Calendar events. Google sign-in lets each account holder choose whether to authorize access.

Your data

Mail and calendar requests go from the desktop client to Google. Credentials are kept in the desktop keyring, and mail is cached locally. Optional AI assistance sends the context you request to the AI provider configured on your desktop. This informational website does not receive your Google credentials or mailbox data.
