Privacy policy

For Tyler Mayberry’s personal Omamail Desktop integration.

Operator and scope

Tyler Mayberry at Animas AI operates this personal OAuth configuration. This policy describes its use with the local Omamail desktop plugin. It does not replace the upstream software’s documentation or Google’s policies.

Data accessed and why

With your authorization, the client accesses Gmail account information, messages, message bodies, attachments and labels to display, search, organize, compose and send mail. It accesses Google Calendar events to display, create, update and delete events at your direction. OAuth tokens maintain the connection to authorized accounts.

Storage and retention

Refresh tokens are stored in the desktop’s GNOME Keyring. Account settings, cached mail, downloaded attachments and any AI conversation records may remain on the desktop until removed or evicted by the client. Local caches are not a promise of encrypted storage. Google retains the original mailbox and calendar data under your Google account settings. This website does not host a mailbox backend or receive Google OAuth tokens.

Sharing and optional features

The client communicates with Google to provide mail and calendar functionality. Sending messages or invitations shares their content with the recipients you select. If you request AI assistance, the selected message or draft context and your request are sent to the AI provider configured in Omarchy; that provider’s terms and retention settings apply. Loading remote images can disclose your IP address and a viewing request to the image host. Ordinary mail and calendar use does not require these optional features.

The operator does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising, data brokerage or training generalized AI models. Google data is used only to provide the user-facing features described here. Omamail Desktop’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Control and deletion

You can revoke Omamail Desktop’s access in Google Account connections and remove accounts in Omamail. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not erase previously cached files. To remove local data, clear Omamail’s cache, downloaded attachments and assistant history, and remove its keyring entries as appropriate. Deleting local data does not delete messages or events stored by Google. Contact the operator for help with local cleanup or privacy questions.

Website visits and changes

Cloudflare serves these informational pages and may process ordinary request metadata, such as IP addresses, for delivery and security. These pages include no analytics scripts, account sign-in form or email collection form. This policy will be updated here if this setup’s data practices change.
